§ 01What we do

The work most AI projects skip.
We start there.

Most companies have everything they need to make AI useful — it's just stuck in PDFs, drawings, spreadsheets, and the heads of their longest-tenured people. We do the unglamorous work to get it out, organize it, and put it where every team in your company can build on it.

01 · Parse

Reach what others can't.

Scanned specs. Handwritten notes. Complex tables. Technical drawings. The documents generic OCR gives up on — we extract in context, with citations back to the source.

02 · Standardize

One foundation. Many apps.

Schemas, vocabularies, governance, access — defined once with your SMEs. Search, RAG, agents, analytics, internal tools — all build on the same substrate.

03 · Build & operate

Ship the proof.

We don't sell the foundation as theory. We build the products on top of it — internal tools, agents, vertical workflows — and run them in production with SLAs.
